He3-Seeker achieves autonomous active exploration and resource prospecting in challenging extra-terrestrial environments.

Abstract

Helium-3 (He-3) prospect exploration on the lunar surface presents significant challenges due to uneven terrain, extreme illumination conditions, and severe communication latency. We present He3-Seeker, an integrated robotic perception, planning, and active exploration framework tailored for planetary surface exploration and spectral resource detection.

Key System Pipeline

1. Active SLAM & Perception

Provides robust 3D mapping and pose estimation under low-texture and uneven lunar terrain using multimodal sensor fusion.

2. Resource Prospecting Planner

Optimizes exploration trajectories by balancing information gain with spectral target likelihoods for efficient He-3 mining site identification.
